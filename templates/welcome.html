Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find quick answers about rooms, PGs, flats, and renting with zero brokerage on RoomNest.

How do I rent a room or PG on RoomNest with zero brokerage?

RoomNest connects room seekers directly with property owners. There are no agents, brokers, or middlemen involved, which guarantees that you pay absolutely zero brokerage commissions on any listed PG, flat, or room.

Are the rental properties and PGs verified?

Yes! Listings on RoomNest undergo verification. Property owners submit document proof which is approved by our admin team. Look for the "🛡️ Verified Owner" badge on property cards.

Do you support PG accommodations for both boys and girls?

Absolutely. We list boys PGs, girls PGs, and co-living spaces in top localities across Mysore, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad. You can easily filter by gender preferences on the search page.
